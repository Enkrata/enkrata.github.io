# Undercrew art, hosted for Notion

These five images are published here for one reason: Notion page covers and
icons need a publicly reachable image URL, and `Enkrata/undercrew` is private,
so nothing in it can be linked. They are hotlinked by the Undercrew tracker and
wiki pages in Notion.

They are copies. The source of truth is `docs/design/` in the game repo, and
the art direction that governs them is `docs/art-direction.md`. Nothing on this
site links to this folder, and no page renders these images.
